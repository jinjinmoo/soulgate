Privacy policy

Soulgate · last updated 9 November 2026

Soulgate is a free browser game. This page explains what the game stores about you and how to erase it.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress is saved in your browser's local storage, along with a random key that lets your browser act for your hunter on the game server. Clearing your browser data removes both.

What is stored on the game server

  • Your hunter name, which other players can see.
  • Your country, as a two-letter code worked out from your internet connection when you play. Other players can see it. Your IP address itself is not stored.
  • Your catches, market listings, cache openings and Gold Rush wins, which appear on public leaderboards and in the shared market.
  • Simple game statistics (souls caught, money earned, hunts, rank) and when you last played.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you do, we receive your Google account's email address and a Google account ID, and we store a copy of your game save with them so you can continue on another device. We ask Google only for basic sign-in information (your name, email address and profile picture, as shown on Google's sign-in screen); we do not keep your name or picture, and we never get your Google password. Your email address is never shown to other players.

What we don't do

There are no ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone. The game runs on Vercel (website hosting) and Supabase (game server and sign-in), which process the data above only to run the game. Country flags are loaded from flagcdn.com.

Erasing your data

In the game, open System › Game and choose Delete my online data. This permanently erases your hunter, catches, listings, cloud save and Google sign-in from the server. Your progress in your own browser stays, and you can keep playing offline.

Children

Soulgate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
